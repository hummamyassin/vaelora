## Practical limits

This is browser GPS with synthetic-browser validation, not a physical-device field
accuracy/battery certification. Reliable background GPS while the screen is locked
is **not guaranteed**. Foreground loss pauses recording; the latest checkpoint
interval may be lost after a crash. History is device-local and subject to browser
storage clearing/eviction. Use one recording tab. Offline weather, AI and new map
tiles require network access. No cloud sync, native tracking or new live Groq
benchmark is claimed. No deployment was performed.

# VAELORA v2.6.0 — Jordan-Wide Outdoor Platform

**Released:** 2 November 2026 · **Production:** [vaelora-chi.vercel.app](https://vaelora-chi.vercel.app) · **Tag:** `v2.6.0`

## Summary

VAELORA grows from Greater Amman to all of Jordan. You can plan by weather anywhere in the country, discover curated places and trails near you, follow a route, and share a story over your own photo. It is still an outdoor fitness product, not a tourism guide. Details: [CHANGELOG](../../CHANGELOG.md), [Jordan-wide platform](../jordan-platform.md), [places and sources](../jordan-places.md).

## Jordan-wide coverage

- Planning, weather, search and discovery cover Jordan through one boundary module built on geoBoundaries data, with governorate labels in English and Arabic.
- Search is Jordan-wide and drops foreign results. Use My Location works anywhere in Jordan.
- Outside Jordan, VAELORA says it currently supports Jordan and never substitutes Amman.
- There are weather reference points in all 12 governorates, and the regional map shows one per governorate.

## Outdoor discovery

- **Library:** 30 curated places across 10 governorates. 11 are VAELORA-reviewed (Amman). 19 come from open data: RSCN reserves, larger town parks, Dana and Mujib trails, Wadi al-Hasa and four Jordan Trail sections. Dynamic search and GPS locations stay forecast cells.
- **Explore:**
  - Near Me Now, ranked by conditions now, activity fit and distance.
  - Filters for time available, type, difficulty, distance, governorate, saved and reviewed.
  - Saved places on the device, and honest empty states.
- **Location-aware lists:** Home's Featured and nearby lists follow the area you are planning for.
- **Route details:**
  - Labelled estimates of distance, time, climb and difficulty, plus the route shape.
  - Weather fit with today's best window, or tomorrow's.
  - A route sketch and elevation profile.
  - Caveats and sources.

## Tracking

- Recording works anywhere and is never interrupted by coverage rules.
- Follow Route shows the planned line, on- or off-route status and the distance left while the screen is on.
- Activities may store GPS altitude and the followed route. Existing history and backups stay valid.

## Share Studio

- A 9:16 story (1080 × 1920) over a photo you choose, drawn on your device.
- The real, privacy-trimmed GPS route, in luminous platinum for runs and luminous olive for walks.
- An activity icon beside the start; distance as the hero figure; one compact row of duration and pace, plus climb only when genuinely recorded.
- The approved V as the only signature: no wordmark, and no RUN/WALK text.
- Five positions and a size control. Native share where the browser supports files, download elsewhere. The classic cards remain.

## Privacy

- Activities, GPS, saved places and photos stay on the device.
- Only coarse forecast cells reach the server.
- Routes, history and photos never reach the AI provider.
- No new secrets or environment variables.

## Languages

- English and Arabic, with intentional RTL across Explore, route details, Follow Route and Share Studio controls.

  - the legacy suite in 7 configurations;

## Known limitations

- Web and PWA recording is foreground-only. Locking the screen or switching apps pauses it, including route following.
- Curated coverage is uneven: Mafraq has none yet, and the open-data entries are not field-checked.
- Elevation is approximate. Story climb appears only when the phone recorded altitude.
- Web Share behaviour varies by device and browser.
